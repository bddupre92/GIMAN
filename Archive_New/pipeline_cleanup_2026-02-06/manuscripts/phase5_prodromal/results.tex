\subsection{Cohort Characteristics and Phenoconversion Rates}
412 prodromal participants (65\% male, age 68.7$\pm$7.4 years, 67\% RBD+, 72\% hyposmic, 11\% GBA+). Baseline UPDRS-III 10.2$\pm$4.3, MoCA 27.9$\pm$1.9. 75/412 (18.2\%) phenoconverted (median time 3.8 years, range 1.1-7.2). 5-year cumulative conversion: 22\%. Converters vs. non-converters: higher baseline UPDRS-III (14.3 vs. 9.1, $p<0.001$), more RBD (89\% vs. 62\%, $p<0.001$), lower DaTscan SBR (1.64 vs. 2.18, $p<0.001$), more GBA+ (21\% vs. 8\%, $p=0.002$).

\subsection{\deepsurv{} Outperforms Cox Models}
\deepsurv{} C-index 0.76 (95\% CI 0.71-0.81) vs. Cox 0.68 (0.62-0.74), $p=0.003$ (DeLong test). Time-dependent AUCs: 2-year (0.74 vs. 0.68), 5-year (0.78 vs. 0.71), 10-year (0.75 vs. 0.69, all $p<0.05$). Calibration: Brier score 0.14 (DeepSurv) vs. 0.19 (Cox). DeepSurv predictions well-calibrated (observed vs. predicted survival curves aligned, $p=0.28$, Hosmer-Lemeshow).

\subsection{Baseline UPDRS-III Dominates Conversion Risk}
Cox model hazard ratios: Baseline UPDRS-III (per 5-point increase) HR 3.8 (95\% CI 2.3-6.2, $p<0.001$), RBD HR 3.2 (1.8-5.7, $p<0.001$), hyposmia HR 2.4 (1.4-4.1, $p=0.002$), DaTscan SBR (per 0.5 decrease) HR 2.8 (1.7-4.6, $p<0.001$), GBA HR 3.5 (1.9-6.4, $p<0.001$), age (per 10 years) HR 1.4 (1.1-1.9, $p=0.02$). MoCA, sex, LRRK2 non-significant.

\deepsurv{} feature importance (permutation-based): Baseline UPDRS-III (importance 0.38), DaTscan SBR (0.24), RBD (0.18), GBA (0.12), hyposmia (0.09). DeepSurv captured nonlinear interactions: High baseline UPDRS-III + GBA mutation synergistically increased risk (interaction HR 6.2, $p=0.008$).

\subsection{High-Risk Stratum Identifies 31\% 5-Year Converters}
Risk tertiles: Low (predicted 5-year conversion <8\%, observed 4\%), Medium (8-18\%, observed 15\%), High (>18\%, observed 31\%) (Figure). High vs. low HR 12.4 (95\% CI 6.1-25.2, $p<0.001$, log-rank). High-risk sensitivity 89\%, specificity 78\%, PPV 31\%, NPV 96\%. High-risk characteristics: Baseline UPDRS 14.8$\pm$2.1, 94\% RBD+, 87\% hyposmic, DaTscan SBR 1.58$\pm$0.24, 28\% GBA+.

\subsection{Trial Enrichment Reduces Sample Size by 42\%}
Standard prodromal trial (all-comers): $N=384$/arm for 80\% power to detect 40\% hazard reduction at 2 years. High-risk enrichment: $N=222$/arm (42\% reduction). High-risk stratum's elevated baseline conversion rate (31\% vs. 22\% overall) amplifies absolute treatment effect. Median trial duration reduced from 2.5 to 2.0 years. Simulation shows 88\% of high-risk prediction accurate at 1-year follow-up (PPV 88\%).
