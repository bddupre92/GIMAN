\subsubsection*{Abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Objective.}
Integrate graph-informed multimodal imputation, gradient-boosted NSD-ISS staging, Graph-Informed Digital Twin transition forecasting, distribution-free conformal uncertainty, and mechanistic dopaminergic-neuron-fraction $N(t)/N_0$ into a single reference-implementation clinical decision-support (CDS) pipeline, and characterize its throughput, per-feature provenance, and uncertainty output on a full longitudinal Parkinson's disease cohort.

\noindent\textbf{Materials and Methods.}
We deployed the pipeline on 1,900 Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative patients with $\geq 2$ staged longitudinal visits. Per patient, the pipeline imputes the 33-feature multimodal vector with stage-conditioned GIMIN under per-feature temperature scaling, predicts NSD-positive stage with CatBoost-12 (trained on PD+Prodromal to address the Espay 2025 reference-class confound), forecasts 15-year cumulative incidence across seven destination stages with the Graph-Informed Digital Twin, attaches IPCW conformal bands, and surfaces $N(t)/N_0$ with 95\% credible intervals from the companion Paper-9 Phase-2 calibration.

\noindent\textbf{Results.}
Pipeline scored 1,900 patients in 22.8\,s (12\,ms/patient, 0 errors). Feature-value provenance was 33.3\% GIMIN, 54.9\% Paper-1 raw, 11.8\% fallback. Within-NSD-positive accuracy: 471/1108 (42.5\%); 792 Stage-0/5/6 patients fall outside scope by design. Mean conformal CIF band width was 0.037 (90\% CL). Mechanistic layer covered 1,065 of 1,900 (56.1\%); median $N(t)/N_0$ = 0.93 (IQR 0.74--0.97).

\noindent\textbf{Discussion.}
The 12\,ms/patient throughput supports interactive EHR-embedded use. Per-feature provenance, complementary temperature-scaled vs.\ conformal uncertainty, and surfaced mechanistic $N(t)/N_0$ jointly address deployment-transparency guidance. Limitations: NSD-positive training scope, transductive GIMIN graph, cross-sectional CatBoost inference, partial (56.1\%) mechanistic coverage.

\noindent\textbf{Conclusion.}
A unified NSD-ISS CDS pipeline with five-layer integration (imputation, staging, transition forecasting, conformal uncertainty, mechanistic $N(t)/N_0$), auditable provenance, and calibrated uncertainty is feasible at 12\,ms/patient. We release it with a pre-registered $n \geq 100$ prospective deployment-audit protocol.

\vspace{0.5em}

\section*{Data Availability}
PPMI data are available to qualified researchers under a Data Use Agreement at \url{https://www.ppmi-info.org/access-data-specimens/download-data}. Upstream artefacts from the companion submissions---NSD-ISS staging tables, multimodal feature files, GIMIN and Graph-DT checkpoints, and IPCW conformal calibration files~\cite{dupre2026paper1,dupre2026paper2,dupre2026paper3,dupre2026paper4,dupre2026paper5}---required to reproduce the pipeline are archived at \url{https://github.com/bddupre92/PD_PHD} and will be deposited to Zenodo with a DOI at acceptance. The full per-patient pipeline output for all 1,900 patients ($\sim 1.2$\,MB aggregate JSON) is included in the supplementary material.

\section*{Code Availability}
Python source for the pipeline (\texttt{scripts/paper6/unified\_pipeline\_demo\_v2.py}), the per-feature temperature-scaling module (\texttt{src/giman\_pipeline/imputation/temperature\_scaling.py}), and the aggregate-figure generator (\texttt{scripts/paper6/generate\_v2\_aggregate\_figures.py}) are openly available at \url{https://github.com/bddupre92/PD_PHD} under the MIT license.
