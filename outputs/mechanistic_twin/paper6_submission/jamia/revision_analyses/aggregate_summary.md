Within the NSD+-evaluable subset (n=1,108; 4-class stages {1, 2B, 3, 4}), the integrated pipeline achieved top-1 accuracy 42.6\% (472/1,108), versus a chance baseline of 25.0\% and a majority-class baseline of 56.0\% (predicting Stage 3 for all patients; n=620/1,108). Top-2 accuracy was 69.7\% (772/1,108), indicating that for the majority of patients the true stage was either the modal or adjacent-modal CatBoost prediction. Per-stage top-1 accuracy: Stage 1 94.4\% (17/18), Stage 2B 39.0\% (46/118), Stage 3 63.9\% (396/620), Stage 4 3.7\% (13/352).

## Paste-ready text for Discussion (coverage selection bias)

Of the 1,900 PPMI patients eligible for the longitudinal pipeline, 1,065 had Phase-2 calibrated posteriors (Wave A n=304; Wave B n=761) and 835 did not, primarily because they lacked $\ge$2 DaT-SPECT scans required for the α-syn/N(t) ODE identifiability. On baseline characteristics, the two subgroups were comparable in age at baseline (covered vs uncovered: 62.6±9.6 vs 62.8±10.7, p=0.664), sex distribution (male %: 62.9\% vs 61.1\%, p=0.441), LRRK2 carrier status (11.8\% vs 7.2\%, p=0.00178), and GBA carrier status (7.6\% vs 6.4\%, p=0.421). As expected by construction, the covered subgroup had more DaT-SPECT scans per patient (2.9±0.9 vs 1.0±0.1, p=0) and longer follow-up (5.7±4.1 vs 3.5±4.1, p=4.09e-31 years; 11.3±6.6 vs 5.6±4.5, p=6.16e-100 longitudinal visits). The NSD-ISS stage distribution was significantly different between subgroups (χ²=433.5, p=1.76e-90), consistent with the fact that patients enrolled into DaT-rich sub-studies tend to cluster in intermediate biological stages. Because the pipeline returns a well-characterised non-mechanistic output for uncovered patients (CatBoost staging + Graph-DT transitions + per-feature conformal bands), this selection pattern affects only which pathway a patient enters, not whether they receive a calibrated prediction.
