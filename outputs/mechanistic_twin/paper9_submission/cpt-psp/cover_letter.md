## Scope and fit

This paper presents the first quantitative systems pharmacology (QSP) analysis to integrate per-patient, imaging-calibrated dopaminergic neuron fraction $N(t)/N_0$ with the full PK/PD response envelope in Parkinson's disease (PD), using 4,203 paired ON--OFF visits from 1,220 Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative (PPMI) patients under a pre-registered three-pathway hypothesis framework. *CPT:PSP*'s mission---quantitative systems pharmacology that links mechanism to clinical outcome---is the natural venue: we deliver a falsifiable, identifiability-aware PK/PD model whose primary finding (positive $N(t) \times \mathrm{LEDD}$ interaction, $p = 0.044$) is clinically actionable and whose two informative negatives scope the limits of the approach.

## Originality and significance

This paper makes four contributions:

1. **Level 2.5 hybrid PK/PD framework.** We combine population-average PK (LEDD) with patient-specific $N(t)/N_0$ estimated from a Phase-2 importance-sampled Bayesian neurodegeneration model (93.75% leave-one-out coverage). This is methodologically intermediate between generic ODE models (Véronneau-Veilleux 2020) and full per-patient PK calibration (precluded here by absence of plasma levodopa measurements in PPMI), and fills a gap noted in Bakshi *et al.* 2018.

2. **Primary positive finding.** After severity control and mixed-effects adjustment, the $N(t) \times \mathrm{LEDD}$ interaction on the ON--OFF UPDRS-III gap is significant ($\beta = -12.57$, $p = 0.044$, $\Delta\mathrm{AIC} = -72$). Fewer surviving neurons $\rightarrow$ smaller treatment benefit; this is directly relevant to clinical dose-response expectations and will appear in the companion Paper 6 CDS pipeline as a deployment-relevant biomarker.

3. **Two informative negatives that scope the approach.** (a) Elapsed time decisively beats $N(t)$ as a univariate predictor of OFF-state UPDRS-III ($\Delta\mathrm{AIC} = +803$); $N(t)$ is approximately a monotonic transform of time. (b) Wearing-off timing is PK-driven, not neurodegeneration-driven (Cox $C$-index $= 0.515$; $\rho = -0.050$, $p = 0.43$). These negatives clarify which clinical endpoints $N(t)$ is fit for.

4. **Sub-EC$_{50}$ linear regime confirmed.** Free Hill coefficient $h = 0.13$ and Hill-model AIC underperformance (vs.\ linear interaction) empirically confirm that PPMI patients sit in the sub-EC$_{50}$ linear portion of the levodopa dose-response curve, constraining future model choice.
